# ProtectSmartly — Family Safety App with One-Tap SOS and Live Location

[ProtectSmartly](https://www.protectsmartly.com) is a family and personal safety app for iPhone and Android. One tap sends an SOS with your location to the people you trust. A shared family map, check-ins, and safe places keep the same circle connected the rest of the time.

## What the app does

| Feature | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [Emergency SOS](https://www.protectsmartly.com/sos-app) | One tap starts a 5-second countdown you can cancel. When it finishes, emergency contacts get your location by email and push notification. |
| [Check-ins](https://www.protectsmartly.com/features) | A short “I’m safe” message, with your location, to the contacts you choose. Check-in history stays in the app. |
| [Live family map](https://www.protectsmartly.com/live-location-sharing) | Current location, battery, and speed for people who accepted your request. Share My Location turns the pin off for everyone. |
| Safe places | Mark Home, School, or Work so the map shows when someone arrives. |
| Footsteps | A member’s path for today, 24 hours, 3 days, or 7 days, grouped into stops. |
| Family messages | Chat with people in your circle from the family map. |
| Driving (beta) | Estimated trips, distance, time, and speed from location readings, for today, 7 days, and 30 days. |
| Local emergency numbers | Police, ambulance, and fire numbers for the country you are in, with a tap to call. |
| Medical details | Blood group, age, height, weight, and allergies, stored on your profile for an emergency. |

Alerts that cannot reach the server while the phone is offline are queued and sent when the connection returns.

## How a circle works

1. Install the app and create an account with email, Google, or Apple.
2. Invite family or friends by email. Nothing about your location is shared until they accept.
3. Choose who counts as an emergency contact for SOS and check-ins.
4. Turn on Share My Location only while you want the family map updated.

SOS and check-ins still reach emergency contacts while a contact request is waiting. Location, Footsteps, and Driving stay hidden until that person accepts.

Setup steps are on [How it works](https://www.protectsmartly.com/how-it-works).

## Privacy

Location is shared only while Share My Location is on, and only with contacts who accepted the request. You can switch sharing off at any time.

Data is encrypted in transit. ProtectSmartly does not sell personal data. End-to-end encryption is on the roadmap and is not live yet.

## Questions people ask

**Is ProtectSmartly free?**  
Yes. The free plan includes SOS, check-ins, a family map, and emergency contacts, with the monthly limits above. Premium removes those limits.

**Do emergency contacts need the app?**  
No. Contacts with an email receive the SOS by email. Contacts who also have the app receive a push notification.

**Can I cancel an SOS?**  
Yes. The button starts a 5-second countdown. Cancel before it finishes and nothing is sent.

**Who can see my location?**  
Only people you invited who accepted the request, and only while Share My Location is on.
